Mylapore

As a teenager he was a Rasika listening to the contemporary great musicians at Kapaleeswarar temple and house concerts in and around Mylapore - a young lad absorbing Carnatic Krithis and their lyrical beauty

A life inside the tradition

Epics, puranas, sthalapuranas and teachings of saints. Decades long association with musicians and dancers. Continued interest in art forms through formal training of daughters in Carnatic music and Bharatanatyam.

1998 - the spark

One of the early lyrics penned was tuned and rendered by the renowned Bay area musician Smt. Asha Ramesh in a temple concert at Concord, CA.
