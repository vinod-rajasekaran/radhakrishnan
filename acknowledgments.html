Acknowledgments

A project of this proportion cannot see the light of the day without the Herculean effort and guidance of several loving individuals who aided in different stages of the project.

My gratitude first for my loving wife, Girija, who steered me into this venture based on my passion for the Tamil language and interest in music. Girija spent countless hours digitizing the songs and envisioning a website.
